# Talking-head mode

## Goal

Keep the speaker's meaning, face, and personality central while preventing visually flat delivery.

## Plan

- Identify hook, framing, strongest claims, examples, proof, emotional turns, and ending.
- Use captions as the main comprehension layer, with visuals supporting specific lines.
- Keep eye line, mouth, gestures, and meaningful props unobstructed.
- Use crop changes and punch-ins sparingly at semantic emphasis points.
- Use split screen, diagrams, screenshots, or full-screen cards only when they add context or proof.
- Maintain continuity across jump cuts; avoid unexplained scale or position changes.
- Preserve intentional pauses and natural delivery.

## Common failures

- face-only footage for too long with no semantic change
- constant punch-ins, bouncing captions, or unrelated b-roll
- captions covering the mouth or hands
- editing every sentence with a different visual system
- removing personality to make speech unnaturally compressed

## Review

Watch once for meaning, once muted for visual clarity, and once focusing only on caption collisions and jump-cut continuity.
